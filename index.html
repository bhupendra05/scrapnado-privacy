SCRAPNADO: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

SCRAPNADO is a single-player game. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no login, and it does not send your personal data, gameplay or device information to us or to anyone else. The Android app contains no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network requests.

What is stored, and where

Your progress (cleared stages, coins, upgrades, skins, missions, daily reward streak, best Endless run and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes it. The Android app turns off cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google backups.

Vibration

The Android app uses your phone's vibration for short feedback when your storm takes a hit or beats a boss. This needs no personal data.

Ads and game portals

The Android app shows no ads. When you play SCRAPNADO on a game website such as CrazyGames, that website may show ads, or offer its own account and cloud-save features, under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play or game-portal page (Taletwist Games).
